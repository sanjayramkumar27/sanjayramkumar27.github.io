Approach

Approach & Interests

I'm broadly interested in robotics and mechatronics, particularly in problems involving the modeling, control, and optimization of physical systems. My experience so far has spanned different domains - from legged locomotion and robotic manipulation to spacecraft attitude control and embedded mechatronic systems. I am open to working on a wide range of robotic platforms and applications.

Across these different systems, my primary interest is in model-based approaches: understanding the underlying dynamics and using optimization, trajectory planning, MPC, QP-based control, or other structured methods to achieve reliable and efficient behavior. I am especially interested in problems where understanding the structure of the physical system can provide an advantage over purely heuristic or black-box approaches.

Legged Locomotion

Trajectory optimization and controller–morphology co-design for dynamic legged systems.

Robotic Manipulation

Optimization-based control, including MPC and classical methods, for robotic manipulators and real-time motion generation.

Space & Mechatronic Systems

Attitude control, embedded systems, and dynamics-based control for spacecraft and other physical systems.

About Me

I'm a final-year Aerospace Engineering student at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST) simultaneously doing an online degree from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras in BS Electronic Systems. My Academic background combines, mechanics, dynamics and control with electronics, embedded systems and signal processing. Over the past few years, I have focused on robotics and mechatronics through hands-on projects involving robot design, control, embedded systems, simulation and optimization. My work has ranged from building physical robots and developing controllers to modelling spacecraft dynamics and working on morphology and trajectory optimization during my internship at ISRO.
